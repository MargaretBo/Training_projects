**Welcome to The Culk**
Nothing interesting here though, just a calculator with some bad spelling.


**Installation**
To install the calculator, run the "make install" command in the terminal window. The Culc.app would then be found in the build folder.

**The following operations and functions are implemented in the calculator:**
Addition - a + b
\n
Subtraction - a - b
\n
Multiplication - a * b
\n
Division - a / b
\n
Power - a ^ b
\n
Remainder of the division - a mod b
\n
Unary plus - +a
\n
Unary minus - -a
\n
To enter complex expressions use parentheses like so - (5+(-7))
\n
You can also use variable "x" for more complex expressions. You can enter a numeric value for x in the text input widget near the "x" button after you click on the widget.
\n
Computes cosine - cos(x)
\n
Computes sine - sin(x)
\n
Computes tangent - tan(x)
\n
Computes arc cosine - acos(x)
\n
Computes arc sine - asin(x)
\n
Computes arc tangent - atan(x)
\n
Computes square root - sqrt(x)
\n
Computes natural logarithm - ln(x)
\n
Computes common logarithm - log(x)
\n
Click "=" and get the answer to your problem. Before entering your expression click on the text input widget at the top.
\n
The calculator implements the possibility of plotting functions.
Enter the expression and click the "Culk" button. It will not break anything, I promise.
After that click on the graph widget. You will be able to change scale using the cursor or the mouse wheel. 
The scope of definition and the scope of function values are limited to at least numbers from -1000000 to 1000000.
